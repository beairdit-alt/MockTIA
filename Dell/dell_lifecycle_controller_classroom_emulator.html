Student Lab

Complete the tasks in the emulator and grade the knowledge check.
Lifecycle Controller Skills Check
  1. Find the log entry showing a storage problem and identify the bay.
  2. Open Firmware Update, choose a local USB repository, and scan for updates.
  3. Select two healthy 600 GB drives and configure RAID 1.
  4. In OS Deployment choose UEFI, Secure Boot enabled, and Windows Server.
  5. Run Hardware Diagnostics and identify the subsystem with a warning.
  6. Explain why firmware compatibility, recovery readiness, stable power, and a maintenance window matter.
1. Which key commonly launches Lifecycle Controller during POST on many PowerEdge systems?
2. Two 600 GB drives in RAID 1 provide about how much usable capacity?
3. A firmware package appears intended for a different PowerEdge model. What should you do?
