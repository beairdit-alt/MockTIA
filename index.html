How to Complete a MockTIA Virtual Lab

1. Choose a lab and enter your full name.

2. Follow the guided tasks or select Assessment Mode if available.

3. Use the simulated workbench and diagnostic tools to complete the objectives.

4. Check your results and select Print / Save Completion PDF.

5. Save your completion report as a PDF and submit it to the correct Canvas assignment.

Note: The tools inside these labs are simulations. They do not change settings on your actual computer, operating system, or network.
