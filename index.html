How to Complete a MockTIA Lab

1. Choose a lab and enter your full name when prompted. 2. Follow the guided tasks, or switch to Assessment Mode if available. 3. Use the simulated workbench and diagnostic tools to finish the objectives. 4. Check your results and use Print / Save Completion PDF to turn in your work through Canvas.

Each lab opens as a standalone HTML page. The tools inside are simulations—not real Windows, Linux, or switch configurations. Your instructor may require additional screenshots or verification where a report cannot determine a score.
