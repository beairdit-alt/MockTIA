How to Complete a MockTIA Virtual Lab

Step 1: Select a lab and click Launch Lab.

Step 2: Enter your first and last name.

Step 3: Follow the guided instructions or choose Assessment Mode when available.

Step 4: Complete the interactive tasks, troubleshooting scenarios, and verification steps.

Step 5: Review your results and select Print / Save Completion PDF.

Step 6: Save the report as a PDF and upload it to the appropriate Canvas assignment.

Important: These are simulated environments. Commands and configuration changes affect the virtual lab, not a real Windows or Linux computer.
