UPS Knowledge

VA is apparent power capacity. W is real power capacity. Both UPS ratings matter, and runtime depends on battery energy and load.

An 865 W rating does NOT mean the UPS can provide 865 W for one hour. Runtime estimates here are illustrative classroom values, not manufacturer specifications.
