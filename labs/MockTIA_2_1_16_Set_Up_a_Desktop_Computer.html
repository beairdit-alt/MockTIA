Technician tip

Click an inventory item, then click the matching connection point in the workbench—or use the labeled ports below.

Safety and connector reference

Connect peripheral cables before turning on power. HDMI carries video; USB connects input devices; RJ-45 connects Ethernet; IEC power cords supply AC power. Never force a connector into an incompatible port.
