2.3.2 Lab: Select and Configure Dual Monitors

Lab Tasks

    WHAT TO DO NEXT

    Work order

    Upgrade the Executive Office PC to two extended 1920 × 1080 displays at 60 Hz. Connect Monitor 1 with HDMI and Monitor 2 with DisplayPort. Monitor 1 is primary and positioned to the left of Monitor 2.

    Student

    Realistic Workstation

    Select HDMI → click PC HDMI → click Monitor 1. Then select DisplayPort → click PC DisplayPort → click Monitor 2. The pictured panel is a visual reference; the marked buttons are the clickable connection targets.

    Cable Inventory

    HDMI: PC HDMI → Monitor 1. DisplayPort: PC DisplayPort → Monitor 2. Each cable must be connected at both ends. VGA is a distractor in this training scenario.
